My Story

My journey into software development started back in college when I built a simple data analysis tool for a local research project. Seeing how code could instantly transform raw data into actionable insights that helped real people was a revelation. I was hooked.

Since then, I have dedicated myself to mastering the Python ecosystem and artificial intelligence. I spent years refining my skills in backend development, machine learning, and eventually, generative AI. Over the last 3 years, I've worked with startups and established tech companies to build scalable AI applications, intelligent agents, and high-performance backend systems.

Today, as a Gen AI & Python Developer, I focus on AI architecture, system optimization, and mentoring junior developers. I love diving deep into LLM internals, exploring advanced RAG strategies, and crafting intelligent workflows that make applications feel alive.
